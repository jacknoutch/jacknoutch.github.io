Since January 2026 [I have been digitising](https://github.com/jacknoutch/north_hillard_latin_prose_composition) the classic Latin Prose Composition textbook, North & Hillard.

Digitising the textbook will allow for a more appropriate route through the material. In book and PDF form, the volume requires a student to constantly flip back and forth between exercise, vocabulary, and other relevant parts of the book. The goal is to collate all necessary material in one place.

In the spirit of learning in public, I am also publishing my own attempts at the exercises and [[North and Hillard Latin Prose Composition Exercises|my commentary on the errors I make]].
